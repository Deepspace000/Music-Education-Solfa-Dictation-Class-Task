Warm-up: sing the scale

Sing along with the solfa. Each note lights up as it sounds.
 

1. Build the rhythm

Click a rhythm card to add it to the next free spot. A card must fit in the bar, so use za (rest) or a longer note to finish a bar.
Rhythm cards

2. Add the solfa melody

Click a note on the staff (it turns blue), then pick its solfa. After each choice the blue note moves on by itself. Keys: d r m f s l t, and Shift+D for high do'. Backspace or Undo last note removes the last solfa you added.
